# Lead List — Miami Jewish Businesses (Outreach Targets)

Researched September 2026 from public sources only. The first three double as the
"concept redesign" case studies on the portfolio site — they have been audited firsthand.
The bonus leads are verified as real businesses but have NOT been fully audited yet;
check their sites before reaching out.

---

## Audited (featured as concept redesigns)

### 1. Pita Loca — Kosher Israeli restaurant
- **Address:** 4017 Prairie Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33140
- **Site:** https://miamibeachkosherrestaurant.com
- **Profile:** Glatt kosher Israeli restaurant, serving Miami Beach since 1997. Friday Shabbat takeout buffet.
- **What's wrong:** Bare-bones template page — a wall of text, no photos, no menu, no online ordering, no clear hours/location block. Generic domain that doesn't match the business name.
- **Outreach angle:** "Your restaurant has 25+ years of reputation and a website that hides it. I mocked up what a proper site could look like — can I show you?"

### 2. Mozart Cafe — Kosher restaurant
- **Address:** 18110 Collins Ave, Sunny Isles Beach, FL 33160
- **Site:** https://mozartcafe.getsauce.com (ordering platform subdomain — no owned website)
- **Profile:** Kosher cafe/restaurant in Sunny Isles Beach with delivery up to 10 miles.
- **What's wrong:** No real website at all — entire web presence is a Sauce-powered ordering page on a third-party subdomain. Zero brand story, zero search visibility; every order reinforces the platform's brand instead of theirs.
- **Outreach angle:** "Right now customers find Sauce, not Mozart Cafe. A flagship site on your own domain with ordering built in would change that — I put together a concept."

### 3. Bagel Time Cafe — Kosher bagel cafe
- **Address:** 3915 Alton Rd, Miami Beach, FL 33140 · (305) 538-0300
- **Site:** bageltimecafemiami.com (basic) — web presence scattered across Uber Eats, NetWaiter, and directory listings
- **Profile:** Beloved kosher breakfast/brunch institution; strong reviews (4.6, 2000+ ratings on delivery apps). Active Instagram: @bageltimecafe.
- **What's wrong:** No owned flagship experience — ordering is fragmented across third-party apps, no order-ahead for the morning rush, catering/shiva platters buried or missing.
- **Outreach angle:** "Your morning rush deserves order-ahead, and your catering platters deserve their own page. I mocked up a concept — 15 minutes to walk you through it?"

## Outreach notes
- Lead with the free 15-minute site audit, not a pitch. Reference something specific you noticed on their site.
- Best first touch: short, personal email or Instagram DM + the concept mock as proof of work.
- Never claim the concept redesigns are client work — the site labels them clearly, keep it that way in outreach too.
